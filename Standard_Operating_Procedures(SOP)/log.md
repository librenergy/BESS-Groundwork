# Log

## 2026-08-17

* **Category established.** `Standard_Operating_Procedures(SOP)/` created as the home for procedures issued to operating parties and counterparties — the third output type beside the studies in `Work_Product(WP)/` and the generated deliverables in `Data_Product(DP)/`. Placed at top level rather than nested under `Work_Product(WP)/` because SOPs have a different lifecycle: they are **controlled documents** that live on after issuance (revision, effective date, distribution list, acknowledgement, review cadence), whereas a work product is finished when delivered. Index seeded with the recurring procedure set and two structural rules: (1) SOPs **reference** the ECP for notification chains and the Contact Register for numbers rather than restating them, so an ECP change does not silently invalidate every procedure; (2) the repo holds the source of truth and each issuance is logged here with revision, date, and recipient — the evidence record if a dispute turns on which procedure a party was operating to. Several SOPs exist to satisfy the owner-side warranty conditions in the WOM and should cite the clause they protect. No procedure templates written yet. Adopted from a project clone where the category structure was first worked out.
