# Working Backlog

The maintainer's running task list. How to use it:

- One checkbox per task; add context inline (links, dates, who is waiting on it).
- Keep it repo-wide. An item that belongs to one document goes in that folder's `todo.md`; move items there once they attach to a document.
- Check items off in place, or delete them. Anything worth remembering afterwards goes to `log.md`.
- Date items you will care about later: `(added {{DATE}})`.
