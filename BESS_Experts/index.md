# BESS Experts

The training and reference material behind this toolkit, kept as its own resource because it grows on its own clock: new modules, new series, and eventually the community at **bessexperts.com**. Nothing here is project material, and nothing here is contractual: where a reference and an executed agreement disagree, the agreement governs; where a reference and a calculation sheet disagree, the calc sheet governs.

The methodology is defined once and reused. The availability method taught in Module 1 is implemented in the [Daily Performance Report §2](/Data_Product%28DP%29/Daily_Performance_Report/daily-performance-report.md) and carried into the [Metrics Tree's calculation sheets](/Metrics_Tree%28MT%29/calculations/index.md), so the same numbers serve the daily report, the monthly pack, and any liquidated-damages argument.

## Documents

* [BESS Performance Engineering Video Series](bess-performance-engineering-video-series.md) - Episode guide to the video series behind the methodology, with the slide decks committed beside it and a map from each episode to where its content is implemented in this repo.

## Slide decks

Committed so an agent can read the source without leaving the repo. The videos carry the explanation; the decks carry the formulas.

* [M01 E01 - Introduction to BESS Availability Types](m01-e01-introduction-to-bess-availability-types.pdf) - The OBE framework, capacity types, and the SOC basis problem.
* [M01 E02 - Equipment Availability Deep Dive](m01-e02-equipment-availability-deep-dive.pdf) - Status-signal measurement and the string, bus, and site roll-up.
* [M01 E03 - Operational Availability Deep Dive](m01-e03-operational-availability-deep-dive.pdf) - Shortfall, the ramp-widened dead-band, and the directional SOC exclusion.
* [M01 E04 - Power Availability Deep Dive](m01-e04-power-availability-deep-dive.pdf) - ACP and ADP, the weakest-string roll-up, and the rail-aware combination.
* [M01 E05 - Energy Availability Deep Dive](m01-e05-energy-availability-deep-dive.pdf) - Capacity retention as a scalar, why per-interval layering double-counts, and how to read a capacity test. (The toolkit now calls this metric Energy Capacity; the episode keeps its original title.)
* [M01 E06 - How to Process your Availability Data](m01-e06-processing-availability-data.pdf) - The OBE combination and the path from EMS to reported number.
