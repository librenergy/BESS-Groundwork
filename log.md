* **Energy availability corrected, and Module 1 completed.** The E05 deck arrived after the OBE implementation and **corrected** the draft rather than extending it: energy availability is measured by a controlled capacity test, so its accuracy is the *highest* of the four types and its update frequency the lowest, the reverse of what the summary table in E01 implied. * **Availability methodology adopted repo-wide: the OBE framework.** The Daily Performance Report's §2 is now built on **OBE (Overall Battery Effectiveness)**, the BESS analogue of manufacturing's OEE, implemented from the maintainer's Module 1 presentation series. Four availability types, each blind where the others see: **Equipment** (on/off, full calendar coverage), **Operational** (meter vs setpoint, most accurate but only while dispatched), **Power** (full coverage from an EMS estimate), **Energy** (MWh, weakest data foundation). Combined as the average of the per-interval **minimum** rather than a product or an average, because the four are not independent failure modes. Each type now carries its computation from the signal level up, including the two rules that most implementations get wrong: the **directional SOC exclusion** in operational availability (a shortfall is excused only when the battery hit a physical limit in the *same direction* as the request), and the **weakest-string roll-up** in power availability (`min(String_AXP) × connected strings`, not the sum, because strings on a common DC bus are voltage-locked). Power availability is defined as `min(ACP, ADP)` away from the SOC rails and single-direction at them, which is the formulation the toolkit now recommends **negotiating into service agreements**; the `max(charge, discharge)` alternative found in many agreements cannot see a one-sided derate and is flagged as the formulation to refuse. Capacity normalisation to the POI became a first-class subsection (apparent-power vs active-power rating, directional losses, sustained C-rate). Conversely §3 was **reduced** to a transcription frame: offtake availability definitions have no market standard worth reproducing, so the template now says "based on the executed tolling agreement" and supplies a fill-in table plus the two questions that decide whether the daily report can shadow the number at all. Glossary gained OBE, EA, OA, PA, QA, ACP/ADP and Overbuild. Details in the [folder log](Data_Product%28DP%29/Daily_Performance_Report/log.md).
