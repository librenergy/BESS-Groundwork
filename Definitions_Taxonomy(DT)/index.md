# Definitions & Taxonomy

Global glossary and naming authority for the project repo: every acronym and term used anywhere in the documentation, plus the conventions for naming and coding organizations, equipment, and generated-document IDs.

* [Definitions](definitions.md) - Global glossary template: every acronym, term, and organization used across the repo, with project-specific meaning and ambiguous terms flagged.
* [Taxonomy](taxonomy.md) - Naming and coding conventions template: canonical equipment/system terms, organization codes, prefix/ID schemes for generated documents.
