# IT Systems Reliability Study

Reliability check of the site's IT, network, and communications infrastructure — a walk from the ISP demarcation inward through every device carrying site data or control traffic, testing each one for redundancy, spares, configuration recoverability, and the named people who can rebuild it.

Scoped as a **quick annual exercise** rather than a design review: wide and shallow by default, going deep only on the components that fail the four questions. Its findings feed the spare parts review, the SOP set, and the failover test study.

* [it-systems-reliability-study.md](it-systems-reliability-study.md) - The study template: the four questions, the traffic-path walk, component inventory across seven zones (WAN, security edge, core & field network, control & telemetry, compute & storage, power, environment & time), cross-cutting checks, findings, and the outage-time failure lookup table.
