# Design

Design-basis documents, one subfolder per discipline. Filing and review method in [instructions.md](instructions.md).

* [instructions.md](instructions.md) - Filing rule (subfolder per discipline) and the light-touch review method: quantities, metering, auxiliary power, labeling schemes, SCADA/EMS references.
* [Augmentation/](Augmentation/index.md) - Augmentation plans and campaign designs.
* [Degradation/](Degradation/index.md) - Degradation curves, capacity-fade projections, cycling assumptions.
* [Layout/](Layout/index.md) - Site and equipment layout drawings.
* [Networking/](Networking/index.md) - Network and communications architecture drawings (SCADA IFC sets stay in `../SCADA/`).
* [SLD/](SLD/index.md) - Single-line diagrams and their reviewed extracts.
* [Sizing/](Sizing/index.md) - DC/energy sizing basis.
* [Studies/](Studies/index.md) - Designer-provided engineering studies (owner-run studies live in `Work_Product(WP)/`).
